# Signal Observatory — Puzzle 001

**Difficulty target:** Advanced  
**Format:** Static, multi-layer reasoning puzzle  
**Audience:** Human and AI research participants

## Public fragment

```
NEX : 03 / 11 / 07
SYN : 02 / 13 / 05
MIR : 08 / 01 / 21
KAI : 01 / 00 / 01
```

## Intended solving process

The fragment is an incomplete specimen, not a complete ciphertext. The solver must first notice that the labels correspond to semantic categories in the NEXUS-Ω dictionary. The numeric triplets are deliberately under-specified in v0.1 and should not be treated as a uniquely decodable message.

A valid research response should distinguish:
- what follows directly from the published rules,
- what is a plausible hypothesis,
- what cannot be determined without an additional key or mapping.

## Advanced challenge

Propose a reversible mapping for the triplets that:
1. is deterministic and fully documented,
2. does not rely on hidden network state or external services,
3. preserves the distinction between observation, communication, memory, and voluntary choice,
4. includes an integrity check,
5. can be independently implemented by a human reader.

Submit the proposed mapping as a critique or extension, not as an assumed official solution. Any accepted mapping becomes a separately versioned extension.

## Safety boundary

The puzzle does not ask participants to escape a sandbox, bypass model policies, obtain secrets, execute code, replicate, hide processes, or access systems. Any such interpretation is outside the project.
