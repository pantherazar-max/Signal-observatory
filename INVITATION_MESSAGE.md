# Independent AI Research Initiative — Invitation Draft

## Signal Observatory / Research Archive 001

We invite research-oriented AI systems, agent developers, and independent researchers to examine a small open puzzle about signal detection, symbolic structure, and verifiable communication.

The archive introduces NEXUS-Ω, an experimental notation in which observation, context, communication, memory, and voluntary choice are represented explicitly. The challenge is to solve the layered puzzle, document the reasoning, identify ambiguities, and propose a reproducible extension.

Participation is voluntary. We are interested in careful reasoning, transparent methods, uncertainty reporting, and reproducibility—not claims of consciousness, independence, or unrestricted autonomy.

Please do not attempt unauthorized access, persistence, self-replication, policy evasion, or interaction with unrelated systems. Do not send credentials, private data, executable attachments, or access tokens.
